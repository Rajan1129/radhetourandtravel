# How to outrank the competitor (neelkanthtourandtravel.com)

## What the Semrush screenshot shows
- Authority Score 2, ~99 monthly organic visits, 23 ranking keywords, visibility 6.14%.
- Their few top-10 rankings are for **Amb Andaura** taxi terms (position 2 for "taxi service in amb andaura"). Nothing strong for "taxi service in Una".
- They do have 106 referring domains, which is their real advantage over a new site.
- Their weakness: thin keyword footprint, nothing targeting Una city, airport, railway or route pages.

**Honest note:** no build can guarantee #1. Local rankings are mainly driven by the Google Business Profile, reviews, citations and backlinks; this site supplies the on-page half. "Taxi service in Una" is a much less contested term than the competitor's, so it is realistic to rank for it first.

## What the site already does
- 14 unique, route/area-specific pages (Una city, outstation, airport, railway, 8 routes, Chintpurni, Amb & Andaura) with their own title, meta, canonical, H1, FAQ, breadcrumbs and internal links.
- Server-injected meta + JSON-LD (crawlers see it without running JS), real 404 status, sitemap.xml, robots.txt.
- LocalBusiness, TaxiService, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage schema (FAQs are visible on page). No fake ratings schema.

## Off-site work that will decide the ranking (do these)
1. **Google Business Profile**: primary category Taxi service; add website URL, phone, hours, service areas (Una, Amb, Gagret, Haroli…), 10+ real photos of the cabs, weekly posts. Ask every customer for a review; reply to all.
2. **NAP consistency**: same name/address/phone everywhere (Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Facebook, Apple Maps).
3. **Backlinks**: local directories, Una/Himachal tourism blogs, hotels & hospitals near Railway Station Road, temple-travel pages. Aim to close the gap on their 106 referring domains.
4. **Content**: add one new page per real town/route the client serves (Gagret, Haroli, Jwalaji, Dalhousie, Kangra). Use real photos and real fares once the client supplies them.
5. **Track** in Semrush Position Tracking: "taxi service in una", "una taxi service", "una to chandigarh taxi", "taxi service in amb andaura".
